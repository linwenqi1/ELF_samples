## 📝 Analysis Summary
> This is a Mirai botnet variant that attempts to establish persistence by modifying system initialization files and spreading copies of itself to multiple temporary directories. The malware executes from /tmp, spawns multiple shell processes to run utilities (pidof, cat, cp, rm, touch, grep), and writes ELF content to files, indicating self-replication. It attempts to access /etc/inittab (though missing in this environment) and repeatedly tries to delete /etc/inittab2, suggesting cleanup of previous infection attempts. The pattern of writing '0:2345:respawn:/tmp/[binary_name]' to files is characteristic of Mirai's persistence mechanism via inittab modification.
